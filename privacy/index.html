Privacy

Almost Heaven Critter Jump is a West Virginia critter peg-jump puzzle by Jupiter Rockets, for kids and families ages 6 and up.

The game collects no personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. Scores, settings, and lesson progress stay on this device and are not sent to Jupiter Rockets.

The band video, the banjo tune, and the spoken lines play from files stored with the game. The game does not download anything extra while you play.

Jupiter Rockets publishes this page for the public web game.
